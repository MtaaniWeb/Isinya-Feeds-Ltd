About Isinya Feeds Ltd

Supporting Farmers With Quality Poultry Products

At Isinya Feeds Ltd, we are committed to supporting farmers with quality poultry products and practical farming solutions.

Located in Kisaju, Kajiado County along the Nairobi-Namanga Road, we provide healthy improved kienyeji chicks, commercial layers, and other poultry solutions designed to help farmers build successful poultry businesses.

Our goal is simple: provide reliable birds, professional guidance, and dependable service that helps farmers achieve better growth, productivity, and returns.
